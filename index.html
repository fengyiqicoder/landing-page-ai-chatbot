Yes, 14 days, and you can cancel any time before it ends without paying anything.

Which apps does it work with?

iPhone, Android, Mac and the web. Your notes sync across all of them.

Who can hear my recordings?

Nobody. Audio is deleted after it is transcribed, and notes are encrypted at rest.
